Bhargavi ERP

Mobile-First Accounting & ERP

Under Development

About

Bhargavi ERP is a professional-grade accounting and ERP app designed for small businesses. Built with modern tools like Drift (SQLite) and Riverpod for a fast, reliable experience.

Planned Features

  • Double-Entry Bookkeeping — Professional accounting with proper journal entries
  • GST Compliance — Auto-calculate GST, generate invoices
  • TDS Management — Track TDS deductions and filings
  • PDF & Excel Export — Generate reports and statements
  • HRM Features — Camera integration, GPS tracking for employees
  • Biometric Auth — Secure login with fingerprint/face
  • 100% Offline — Works without internet, syncs later

Development Status

Core architecture designed and database schema built. UI/UX and business logic in progress.
