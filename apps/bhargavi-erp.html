Bhargavi ERP

School management - fees, attendance, reports

Built - Pre-Release

About

Built a complete school management system for Bhargavi. Handles fee collection, student attendance, exam results, and generates detailed reports - designed for non-technical school staff in small towns.

Student Management

  • Student registration with class and section assignment
  • Roll number auto-generation and manual override
  • Student profile with photo, parent info, and contact details
  • Active/inactive status toggle for transferred students

Fee Collection

  • Monthly fee structure setup per class
  • Fee collection with receipt generation
  • Due tracking with pending amount alerts
  • Concession and discount support
  • Daily collection report and pending dues report

Attendance

  • Daily attendance marking per class
  • Present/Absent/Leave status tracking
  • Monthly attendance summary per student
  • Attendance percentage calculation

Examinations & Reports

  • Exam marks entry for multiple subjects
  • Grade calculation based on marks
  • Report card generation with subject-wise marks
  • Class rank and percentage calculation
  • Printable report cards

Staff & Reports

  • Staff salary management
  • Daily collection and expense reports
  • Student-wise fee reports
  • Class-wise performance reports
