Fasting & Water Tracker

Intermittent fasting timer with hydration tracking

Early Access - Testing

About

Fasting & Water Tracker turns your Android phone into a personal intermittent fasting and hydration companion. 100% offline - tracks fasting windows, water intake, and weight trends with live countdowns and metabolic stage timelines. No accounts, no cloud, no ads.

Fasting Timer

  • Start, pause, and end fasts with one tap
  • Live countdown showing hours and minutes remaining
  • Metabolic stage timeline - Fed, Sugar burn, Fat burn, Ketosis, Deep ketosis, Autophagy
  • Persists across app restarts using timestamps, not ticking timers

Fasting Plans

  • Presets: 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, and custom durations
  • One-tap "start now" for any plan
  • Custom hours for personalized fasting windows

Water Tracking

  • Quick-tap +250ml, +500ml, or custom amounts
  • Daily hydration target with progress ring
  • 7-day history with daily breakdown
  • Undo last entry with confirmation

Hydration Reminders

  • Timed local notifications within your drink window
  • Configurable first drink and last drink times
  • Adjustable reminder interval between drinks

Fast-End Reminders

  • Local notification when your chosen fast duration completes
  • Milestone alerts for ketosis and autophagy stages

Weight Tracking

  • Add weight entries in kg or lb
  • Line chart showing weekly and monthly trends
  • Track progress alongside your fasting journey

Settings

  • Customize water target (ml or oz)
  • Set default fasting plan
  • Configure reminder times and notification preferences
  • Toggle between metric and imperial units

Requirements

  • Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher
  • Notifications permission for reminders
