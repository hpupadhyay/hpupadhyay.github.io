Fasting & Water Tracker

Healthy Fasting & Hydration Habits

Under Development

About

Fasting & Water Tracker is an offline-first app that helps you build healthy intermittent fasting habits and track your daily water intake. Simple timers, progress charts, and reminders — all stored locally on your device.

Planned Features

  • Intermittent Fasting Timer — 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, custom fasting windows
  • Water Intake Tracker — Log glasses, set daily goals, reminders
  • Progress Charts — Visualize your fasting and hydration streaks
  • Notifications — Fasting start/end alerts, water reminders
  • 100% Offline — All data stored on your device

Currently in early development. Join the tester's group to be notified when it's ready for testing.
