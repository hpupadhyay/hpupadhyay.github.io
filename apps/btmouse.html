BTMouse

Turn your phone into a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard

Built - Pre-Release

About

BTMouse turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth mouse, keyboard, and presenter remote for your PC. Useful when your mouse dies or you need a quick pointing device. Works over standard Bluetooth HID protocol - no PC software needed.

Mouse Mode

  • Touchpad-style mouse control
  • Left and right click buttons
  • Scroll gesture support
  • Adjustable sensitivity settings
  • Multi-touch gesture support

Keyboard Mode

  • Full keyboard input over Bluetooth
  • Function keys and special characters
  • Media controls - play, pause, volume
  • Custom keyboard shortcuts

Presenter Mode

  • Slide navigation - next, previous
  • Laser pointer simulation on screen
  • Slide progress indicator
  • Timer display for presentation management

Connection

  • One-tap connect to paired devices
  • Auto-reconnect on startup
  • Works with any Bluetooth-enabled PC
  • No PC software or drivers required
