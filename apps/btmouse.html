BTMouse

Turn your phone into a Bluetooth mouse, keyboard & remote for your PC

Early Access - Testing

About

BTMouse turns your Android phone into a powerful wireless Bluetooth mouse, keyboard, and remote control for your PC. 100% serverless - works directly over Bluetooth with no internet, no accounts, and no ads. No PC software or drivers required.

Mouse & Trackpad

  • Precision trackpad with left and right click
  • Adjustable pointer and scroll speed
  • Air Mouse - tilt your phone to control cursor via accelerometer
  • Swap buttons for left-handed use
  • Dedicated horizontal scroll zone

Full Keyboard

  • Complete PC keyboard with F1-F12, navigation cluster, modifiers
  • Windows and Mac layout toggle (Ctrl/Win/Alt vs Ctrl/Cmd/Opt)
  • Long-press key repeat with adjustable delay and rate
  • Voice-to-text input - speak and type to your PC
  • Paste to PC - reads phone clipboard and types it automatically

Indian Language Keyboard

  • 10 languages: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi
  • Phonetic typing - type how you pronounce

Number Pad

  • Dedicated number pad layout with Num Lock toggle
  • Arithmetic operators and Enter
  • Perfect for data entry and spreadsheets

Programmable Shortcuts

  • Grid of customizable shortcut buttons
  • 12 pre-loaded shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Alt+Tab, Alt+F4, Ctrl+Alt+Del, etc.)
  • Create your own with custom labels and any key combination
  • Long-press to edit, delete with confirmation

Presenter Mode

  • Slide navigation - next, previous
  • Start slideshow (F5), end show (Esc), blank screen (B)
  • Annotation tools - pointer (Ctrl+P) and eraser (Ctrl+E)

Multimedia Controls

  • Play, pause, previous/next track, stop
  • Volume up/down and mute
  • Fullscreen toggle (F11)
  • Large one-handed buttons

Lock/Unlock PC

  • Remotely lock Windows (Win+L), Mac (Ctrl+Cmd+Q), or Linux (Ctrl+Alt+L)
  • Unlock with biometric fingerprint verification
  • Encrypted password storage (AES-256-GCM) - never leaves your device
  • Ctrl+Alt+Del support for Windows secure attention

Clipboard Sync

  • Phone to PC - paste text and it types automatically with progress bar
  • PC to phone - embedded web server with PIN authentication
  • 6-digit session PIN with rate limiting and 10-minute timeout

Mouse Jiggler

  • Keep-Alive foreground service prevents PC from sleeping
  • Subtle 2-pixel movements every 15 seconds with net-zero displacement
  • Perfect for long downloads, RDP/VNC, and keeping status active

Customizable Settings

  • Pointer speed (0.3x to 3x) and scroll speed (0.3x to 3x)
  • Air Mouse sensitivity (1x to 10x) with dead zone filtering
  • Key repeat delay (100-1000ms) and repeat rate (20-200ms)
  • Haptic vibration feedback toggle
  • Keep screen on, auto-connect on startup
  • 3 themes: Light, Dark, System

Requirements

  • Android 9.0 (API 28) or higher
  • PC with Bluetooth support
  • Works with Windows, macOS, and Linux
