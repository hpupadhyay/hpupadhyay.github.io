Ration Karo

Compare Grocery Prices Across Apps

Under Development

About

Ration Karo helps you find the best grocery prices across quick commerce apps like Blinkit, Zepto, and more. Compare prices instantly, get alerts for deals, and save money on every purchase.

Planned Features

  • Price Comparison — Compare prices across Blinkit, Zepto, and other platforms
  • Location-Based Search — Results based on your delivery area
  • Price Alerts — Get notified when prices drop
  • Shopping Lists — Create lists and compare total basket prices
  • Price History — Track price trends over time
  • SQLite Local DB — Fast searches, offline product catalog

Development Status

UI/UX design and data architecture in progress. API integration research ongoing.
