About

Compare grocery prices across Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket and more. Find the cheapest option for your monthly ration with a smart cart optimizer that suggests the best platform for each item.

Smart Cart

  • Delivery fee and minimum order comparison

  • Monthly spending summary by platform
  • Favourite items list with quick reorder
