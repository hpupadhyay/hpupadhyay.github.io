Habit Tracker

Build streaks, track consistency, stay focused

Planned

About

A minimal habit tracker focused on streaks and consistency - not feature bloat. Simple enough to use daily, powerful enough to show real progress over time. Built with Flutter for cross-platform support.

Habit Management

  • Create custom daily or weekly habits with icons and colours
  • Set habit frequency - daily, specific days, or weekly targets
  • Category grouping for better organization
  • Active/inactive toggle for pausing habits

Streak Tracking

  • Current streak count with visual indicator
  • Best streak (longest ever) tracking
  • Completion rate percentage
  • Streak recovery after missed days

Calendar & Visualization

  • Monthly calendar heatmap view
  • Colour-coded completion days
  • Weekly and monthly streak reports
  • Progress charts over time

Reminders & Widgets

  • Customizable daily reminders per habit
  • Home-screen widgets for quick check-in
  • Notification scheduling

Data & Backup

  • Local data storage - no account required
  • Data backup and restore
  • Export habit data as CSV
