About

Comprehensive offline TDS calculator for India covering all provisions under the Income Tax Act, 2025 (effective 1 April 2026). Built on the official TDS Calculator from the Income Tax Department - 30+ sections, salary regime comparison, and a searchable rate chart.

Non-Salary TDS Calculator

  • 30+ TDS sections - EPF, insurance commission, brokerage, rent, property, mutual funds, interest, contractors, professionals, dividends, e-commerce, crypto, lottery, cash withdrawal, and more
  • 7 deductee types - Individual, HUF, Domestic Company, Foreign Company, Firm/LLP, AOP/BOI/Trust, Government
  • Residential status - Resident, Non-Resident (NRI), Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident
  • PAN available toggle - applies Section 397 higher rate when PAN is missing
  • Annual aggregate amount for threshold checking across the full financial year
  • Custom TDS rate override for lower deduction certificates under Section 395
  • Senior citizen toggle for FD/Bank Interest sections with higher thresholds
  • ITR filing status toggle for Cash Withdrawal section with dual-slab calculation
  • Payee surcharge category - eight categories with marginal relief
  • Full result breakdown - gross, rate, TDS, surcharge, cess, net payable, effective rate

Salary TDS Calculator

  • Old Regime vs New Regime side-by-side comparison
  • Standard deduction - Rs.75,000 (New) / Rs.50,000 (Old)
  • Age category - Below 60, Senior (60–80), Super Senior (80+) with different slabs
  • Old Regime deductions - Section 123 (80C), 124 (80CCD(1B)), 126 (80D), 129 (80E), 133 (80G), 153 (80TTA), 22 (24(b))
  • HRA exemption calculator - actual HRA, rent minus 10% basic, 50%/40% of basic
  • Rebate - New Regime: full rebate up to Rs.60,000 (income ≤ Rs.12L); Old Regime: up to Rs.12,500 (income ≤ Rs.5L)
  • Surcharge with marginal relief for residents and non-residents
  • DTAA disclaimer for non-resident employees
  • Tax rounding to nearest Rs.10 per Section 516

Rate Chart & Search

  • Searchable reference chart of all 30+ TDS sections
  • Category filter chips - EPF, Commission, Rent, Property, Interest, Contractor, Professional, and more
  • Each card shows - section name, old/new numbers, rate with/without PAN, company/firm rate, threshold
  • Searchable section picker in calculator with instant filtering

Result Display

  • Full breakdown - gross amount, section info, threshold, rate, TDS, surcharge, cess, net payable, effective rate
  • Contextual warnings for no-PAN penalty and non-resident DTAA
  • Indian Rupee formatting with comma separation
