TDS Calculator India

Calculate TDS deductions as per Indian tax rules

Get on Play Store

About

TDS Calculator India helps you quickly calculate Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) for various payment categories as per Indian Income-tax rules. Useful for accountants, businesses, and salaried professionals.

Features

  • TDS calculation for multiple section categories
  • Support for resident and non-resident rates
  • PAN-based TDS rates (lower/higher deduction)
  • Surcharge and cess calculations
  • Quick and accurate results
  • Fully offline — no internet required
  • Simple, clean interface
