Expense Tracker

Smart Personal Finance Manager

Early Access - Testing

About

Full-featured offline personal finance manager - double-entry accounting system that tracks income, expenses, credit cards, loans, investments, budgets, and net worth. All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no sign-in required.

Account Management

  • Seven account types - Bank, Cash, Wallet, Credit Card, Investment, Liability, Other
  • Full chart of accounts with balance tracking, icons, and colours
  • Account ledger with transaction history per account
  • Active/inactive toggle and custom sort order

Transactions

  • Three types - Expense, Income, and Transfer
  • Multi-line transactions - split across multiple categories and accounts in one entry
  • Merchant tracking with autocomplete from previous entries
  • Payment method, notes, and full-text search
  • Haptic feedback on key actions

Credit Card Management

  • Credit limit, billing day, due day, statement day, minimum payment percentage
  • Available credit and utilization percentage tracking
  • Monthly CC statement - billing cycle visualization, opening balance, category breakdown
  • Minimum vs full payment comparison with balance after payment
  • Due date warnings on home screen for upcoming CC dues

Loan Tracking

  • Borrowed and Given loans - auto-creates linked Liability/Asset accounts
  • Six loan types - Personal, Home, Bike, Car, Education, Other
  • Four interest types - Reducing balance, Simple interest, No interest, Pay at end
  • EMI schedule with principal/interest breakdown, custom payments, and foreclosure
  • CSV import for EMI schedules from spreadsheets
  • Progress tracking with visual progress bars

Financial Reports

  • Income Statement - income vs expenses with category breakdown
  • Balance Sheet - assets, liabilities, equity with retained earnings
  • Cash Flow Statement - operating, investing, financing activities
  • Cash Flow Forecast - 3/6/12 month projections using historical averages
  • CC Statement Report and Merchant Report
  • Charts - monthly cash flow bars, expense breakdown donut, net worth trend

Budget & Recurring

  • Monthly spending limits per category with progress bars and over-budget warnings
  • Recurring transactions auto-generate on schedule and feed into forecasts
  • Fixed expenses tracked separately with forecast integration

Export & Backup

  • Multi-sheet CSV export - Expenses, Income, Transfers, EMI Payments, Given Loans
  • Monthly or all-time export with share integration
  • Local backup - survives uninstall via Android media directory
  • Google Drive backup with auto-backup (hourly/daily/weekly/monthly)
  • Onboarding restore - fresh install scans for existing backups

Settings & Customization

  • Eight currencies - INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, KRW, VND, THB
  • Configurable decimal places synced with currency
  • Light, Dark, and System Default themes with Material 3
  • Books Beginning Date - set a financial epoch for all reports
  • Full category management - edit, delete, group, and reorder
