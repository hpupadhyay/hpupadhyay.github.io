Get on Play Store

About

Expense Tracker is a simple, offline-first app to manage your daily finances. All your data stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud, no sign-in required.

Features

  • Add income and expense entries quickly
  • Organize by categories
  • View daily, weekly, and monthly summaries
  • Export data to CSV
  • Fully offline — your data never leaves your device
  • Dark mode support
