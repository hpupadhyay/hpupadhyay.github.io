Expense Tracker

Smart Personal Finance Manager

Early Access — Testing Join Tester's Group

About

Expense Tracker is a 100% offline personal finance manager that helps you track expenses, manage accounts, and take control of your money. All data stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud, no sign-in required.

Features

  • Account Management — Bank, Cash, Wallet, Credit Card, Investment & Liability tracking
  • Transaction Tracking — Expenses, Income & Transfers with multi-line support
  • Credit Card Management — Statement cycles, due dates, payment tracking & category breakdown
  • Loans & EMIs — Loan taken & given tracking with EMI scheduling
  • Forecasting — Monthly cash flow forecast with opening balance, expenses, EMIs & investments
  • Balance Sheet — Assets, Liabilities & Equity with opening balance support
  • Reports & Charts — Daily/Weekly/Monthly expense charts, merchant reports & CC statements
  • Data Export — Multi-sheet CSV export (Expenses, Income, Transfers, EMIs, Given Loans)
  • Backup & Restore — Local backup that survives uninstall + optional Google Drive backup
  • Budget Management — Set monthly budgets with progress alerts
  • Recurring Transactions — Daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly recurring entries
  • Dark & Light Theme — Multiple currency support
  • 100% Offline — All data stored locally via SharedPreferences

Backup & Restore

Backups are saved to your device's shared storage so they survive app uninstall and reinstall. You can also optionally back up to your personal Google Drive. On reinstall, the app detects existing backups and offers to restore them.
