ArtiStanza Reader

A quiet home for poems and short stories, in your pocket

Early Access - Join the tester group

About

ArtiStanza Reader brings the ArtiStanza library of poems and short stories to Android. Verse is set with every line break left exactly where the author put it, in a members-only library you sign in to. Saving a work keeps a copy on the phone, so a poem still opens on a train with no signal. There are no ads and no tracking, and nothing you read is handed to another app.

Reading

  • Poems and short stories in English, Hindi and Nepali
  • Line breaks, stanza spacing and Devanagari preserved as written
  • A Key List of moods across the top of the feed, to read by feeling rather than by search
  • Works carry their author byline, and open straight into the text
  • Pull to refresh for new work

Offline Reading

  • Save any open work to the phone; it is stored in the app's private storage and opens with no connection
  • A Saved offline shelf lists everything you kept, with remove and clear
  • Saved works survive a restart, and are cleared when you sign out
  • Writes are temp-then-rename, so a half-written file is never left behind

Reading Dashboard

  • Time read - all time, today and this week
  • Current day streak and your daily totals
  • Mood tallies built from what you actually read
  • A session counts once it passes five seconds, so a mis-tap is not counted
  • The whole log lives on your device and is never sent to the server

Kept Private

  • No advertising, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking
  • Published work is protected from screenshots and screen recording
  • No share, copy or export path for verse, which protects the authors you read
  • Backup and device-to-device transfer are excluded for the app's private data

Your Account

  • The library is members-only: sign in or create a free account
  • One session token is kept in secure storage, guarded by the Android keystore; nothing else is persisted
  • Your email, handle and display name are held by the ArtiStanza platform at artistanza.com
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for deletion of your account, by email

Requirements

  • Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher
  • A free ArtiStanza account
  • An internet connection for the library; saved works read offline
