Finance Calculator

Quick financial calculations for everyday use

Get on Play Store

About

Finance Calculator is a multi-purpose financial tool that helps you with EMI, GST, investment, arbitrage, loan comparisons, and more — all in one app.

Features

  • EMI Calculator — calculate equated monthly installments
  • GST Calculator — compute GST amounts instantly
  • Investment Calculator — project returns on investments
  • Arbitrage Calculator — find price differences across platforms
  • Loan Comparison — compare flat vs reducing interest rates
  • Business Calculator — margin, markup, and break-even tools
  • Refinance Calculator — evaluate refinancing options
  • Fully offline — no internet required
