Finance Calculator

16 financial tools for Indian users

Early Access - Testing

About

16 offline financial tools covering loans, EMI, investments, refinancing, arbitrage, debt management, insurance, and more. All calculations performed on-device with PDF export, funnel charts, and calculation history.

Loan & EMI Tools

  • EMI Calculator - monthly EMI, amortization schedule, prepayment savings, moratorium support
  • Loan Amount Calculator - reverse-calculate max loan from desired EMI
  • Tenure Calculator - find repayment period from loan amount and EMI
  • Interest Rate Calculator - reverse-calculate rate using binary search
  • All 4 support processing fee (deducted or added), prepayment, and monthly/yearly views

Investment Suite

  • SIP Calculator - monthly SIP maturity with wealth gain and multiplier
  • Compound Interest - lumpsum maturity with annual compounding
  • FD / RD Calculator - five payout frequencies: At Maturity, Quarterly, Monthly, Half-Yearly, Yearly
  • Inflation Calculator - future value, present value, and purchasing power comparison

Loan Comparison & Decision Tools

  • Compare Loans - side-by-side comparison with EMI, total interest, and recommendation
  • Arbitrage Engine - multi-strategy loan vs investment comparison with 1–5 portfolios
  • Refinance Calculator - current loans vs new plan with foreclosure fees and net savings
  • Refinance vs Business - decision aid with risk tolerance and confidence level
  • Floating to Fixed Rate Converter - break-even analysis and "What if?" scenarios

Planning & Analysis

  • Retirement Planner - corpus needed, monthly savings, sustainable withdrawal
  • Goal Planner - inflation-adjusted goal with SIP/lumpsum needed
  • Debt Payoff Planner - Snowball vs Avalanche vs Minimum strategies
  • XIRR Calculator - annualized return for irregular cash flows
  • Loan Affordability - max loan from income with DTI limit
  • Life Cover Calculator - DIME-based insurance need analysis

Additional Tools

  • Lumpsum Calculator - one-time investment maturity with inflation adjustment
  • CAGR Calculator - compound annual growth rate with absolute return
  • Simple Interest Calculator - basic interest with years or months support

Export & History

  • PDF export on every calculator - formatted results, tables, charts, and formulas
  • CSV export for loan amortization schedules
  • Funnel charts on every result screen - Principal vs Interest, Invested vs Gain
  • Calculation history saved locally - viewable from home screen
  • Currency selection - INR, USD, EUR, GBP with configurable decimal precision
