Restaurant Management

Local-first POS for small restaurants and cafes

Planned

About

A local-first point-of-sale system for small eateries. Your phone acts as the server - handle orders, track tables, generate bills, and view daily sales summaries. Works over Wi-Fi without internet. Designed for quick-service restaurants and small cafes.

Menu & Orders

  • Digital menu with categories and modifiers
  • Order management - dine-in, takeaway, delivery
  • Quick item search and favourites
  • Special instructions and customizations per item

Table Management

  • Table layout with status tracking - available, occupied, reserved
  • Waiter ordering from table-side
  • Kitchen display for order preparation
  • Table transfer and merge support

Billing & Payments

  • Bill generation with multiple payment modes
  • Split bill support
  • Discount and tax configuration
  • Quick checkout with printed receipts

Reports & Inventory

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports
  • Item-wise sales analysis
  • Inventory tracking for key ingredients
  • Low stock alerts
