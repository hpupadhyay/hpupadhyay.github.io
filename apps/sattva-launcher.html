About

A minimal, privacy-focused Android launcher designed for mindful phone usage. One home screen with everything you need - no app drawer clutter, no distraction. Built as a native Android app for maximum performance.

Home Screen

  • Single home screen with your most-used apps
  • Quick app search with instant results
  • Clean, minimal design with no distractions
  • Customizable app grid layout

Screen Time & Focus

  • Screen time tracking with daily limits
  • Focus mode to block distracting apps
  • App usage statistics and insights
  • Daily screen time reports

Widgets & Notes

  • Quick notes widget for jotting things down instantly
  • Clock and weather widget
  • Battery and storage info at a glance
  • Upcoming calendar events widget

Privacy & Performance

  • No data collection - fully offline
  • Lightweight and fast
  • Low battery usage
  • Six language support
