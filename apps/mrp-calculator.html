MRP & Profit Calculator

Import MRP, purchase cost & reverse MRP analyzer

Early Access - Testing

About

Business pricing calculator for importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and online sellers. Calculate MRP using six pricing tools across four import strategies, two local strategies, and two cloud/listing strategies - with reverse analysis, multi-currency support, and PDF export.

Import MRP Calculator

  • Four pricing strategies calculated simultaneously - company margin on Landing/Selling Price × dealer margin on MRP/Selling Price
  • Full breakdown - Base INR, customs, freight, landing cost, company margin, selling price, GST, MRP, dealer invoice, profit per unit
  • Strategy comparison table with Best/Good/Average rank badges
  • Unit-based total profit across all strategies

Local Calculator

  • Two strategies - company margin on Landing Cost vs Selling Price
  • Handles GST-registered and non-registered companies
  • Other expenses - percentage and fixed amount
  • Full breakdown - purchase cost, landing cost, selling price, GST, MRP, profit per unit

Cloud / Listing Price Calculator

  • Two strategies for online marketplace listing price
  • Platform deductions - commission, commission GST, freight, freight GST, total ITC recoverable
  • Reverse calculation of listing price when commission and freight are percentages of the listing price itself
  • GST-registered toggle with Input Tax Credit recovery

Reverse Analyzers

  • Import Cost Analyzer - given a fixed MRP, solve for company margin, dealer margin, or base price
  • Local Cost Analyzer - solve for company margin or purchase cost
  • Listing Price Analyzer - solve for company margin or purchase cost with full platform deduction breakdown
  • Each analyzer works across all applicable strategies

Currency & Tax

  • 17 currencies - INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, BDT, PKR, LKR, NGN, ZAR, KES, CNY, JPY, AUD, CAD, SGD
  • Configurable home currency and import currency with editable exchange rate
  • Customizable tax label - rename to GST, VAT, or any country-specific term
  • GST-registered toggle - ITC on commission and freight GST when registered

Export & History

  • PDF export - professional A4 reports with strategy comparison, breakdown, and recommendations
  • Copy to clipboard - formatted text for messaging and email
  • Calculation history - save, rename, filter, and re-export
  • Strategy selection filter - include only selected strategies in PDF and copy

Formula Guide

  • 4-tab educational guide - Import MRP, Listing Price, Strategy Compare, Visual Flow
  • Step-by-step formulas and color-coded flow diagrams
  • Covers forward and reverse calculations with ITC recovery
