MRP & Profit Calculator

Calculate MRP, profit margins, and discounts

Get on Play Store

About

MRP & Profit Calculator is a quick tool for retailers, wholesalers, and business owners to calculate Maximum Retail Price, profit margins, discounts, and markup percentages.

Features

  • Calculate MRP from cost and margin
  • Profit margin and markup calculator
  • Discount calculator — find final price after discount
  • Break-even analysis
  • Save calculation history locally
  • Support for multiple currencies
  • Clean, ad-free interface
