Income Tax Acts 2025

Complete Income-tax Act and Rules - offline reference

Get on Play Store

About

Complete offline reference for the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Income-tax Rules, 2026. All 522+ sections, 16 schedules, 329+ rules, and 108 embedded tables - extracted from official government PDFs into a fast, searchable, offline reader for tax professionals, CAs, and students.

Bare Act Reader

  • Act / Rules toggle - switch between the complete Act, 2025 and Rules, 2026
  • Chapter-wise navigation with section counts and flat scrollable list
  • Section info table - section number, chapter, acts, year, group displayed above each heading
  • Clean reader with clause-level indentation and bold lead rendering
  • Prev/Next navigation spanning the entire document across chapters
  • Reading progress bar showing scroll position within each section

Bookmarks, Notes & Highlights

  • Bookmark any section with a single tap - saved for quick access
  • Sticky notes pinned inline on specific text - expand/collapse, six colours, edit and delete
  • Text highlighting with eight colours - press-and-drag to select, palette appears automatically
  • Highlights management sheet with per-highlight delete and jump-to-highlight
  • Saved screen with three tabs - Bookmarks, Notes, Highlights - all searchable

Tax Reckoner

  • Quick Reference - categorized tax data: slabs, TDS rates, deductions, thresholds
  • Compare view - select two items and view them side-by-side
  • Search and filter by category with expandable detail cards
  • Each Reckoner item supports its own bookmarks, notes, and highlights

Search

  • Full-text search across all section headings and bodies in both Act and Rules simultaneously
  • Ranked results with contextual body snippets
  • Minimum 2 characters - tap to open directly in the reader

Share & Export

  • Share as plain text - formatted with clause indentation via system share sheet
  • Share as PDF - clean A4 PDF with tables, generated on-device and shared instantly

Appearance & Reading Settings

  • Three app themes - System, Light, Dark
  • Three reading themes - Day, Sepia, Night - independent from app theme
  • Adjustable font size (13–24) and line spacing (Compact, Cozy, Roomy)

Content Updates

  • Check for updates from a public GitHub CDN - diff-based, only changed sections are replaced
  • SHA-256 verification for download integrity
  • Amendment chips in the reader showing kind, author, and effective date
  • Updates & Amendments screen with revision history
