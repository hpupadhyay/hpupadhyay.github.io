About

Income Tax Acts 2025 is a comprehensive offline reference for the Income-tax Act, 2025 (as amended by Finance Act, 2026) and Income-tax Rules, 2026. Ideal for chartered accountants, tax professionals, lawyers, and students.

Features

  • Full text of Income-tax Act, 2025 — 552 sections, 16 schedules
  • Full text of Income-tax Rules, 2026 — 332 rules, 26 tables
  • Offline — no internet required after first load
  • Full-text search across all sections and rules
  • Bookmark sections for quick access
  • Highlight and add personal notes
  • Auto-updates via CDN when new revisions are published
  • Structured tables for easy reference
