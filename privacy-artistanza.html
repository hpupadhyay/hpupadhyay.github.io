Privacy Policy

ArtiStanza Reader  |  Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ArtiStanza Reader mobile application ("the App") handles information. By installing or using the App, you agree to this policy. It covers the App only; the full ArtiStanza platform policy, including the website and creator tools, is published at artistanza.com/privacy.

1. Overview

ArtiStanza Reader is the reading app for poems and short stories published on the ArtiStanza platform. The library is members-only, so reading needs an ArtiStanza account. The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Information held by the ArtiStanza server

The App talks to the ArtiStanza service so it can deliver the works you read. The service holds:

  • Account details: your email address, a handle, a display name, and an optional pen name and tagline. Your password is stored only as a one-way Argon2id hash, never as plain text.
  • Entitlements and reading access: which works your account is entitled to read, so the service can deliver them.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, app type, request identifiers and timestamps, used to keep the service secure, to limit abuse and rate-limit requests, and to diagnose faults.
  • Reader activity on the platform: reading progress, highlights and notes, if you use those features on the website.

3. Information that stays on your device

Two things are kept on the phone and nowhere else:

  • Saved offline copies: any work you save for offline reading, written into the App's private storage. Cleared when you sign out.
  • Reading log: how long you read, which works, and which moods, shown in the reading dashboard. It is never sent to the server.

Both are private to the device and are excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. The App stores exactly one further item, in secure storage guarded by the Android keystore: the session token that keeps you signed in.

4. Advertising and third parties

The App shows no advertising and uses no advertising or analytics SDK. Beyond the ArtiStanza service itself, no third-party SDK collects data through the App.

5. Screenshots and sharing

Published work is protected from screenshots and screen recording, and the App has no share, copy or export path for verse. This protects the authors you read.

6. Service providers

The ArtiStanza service uses providers that process data on its behalf: Supabase (database hosting), Google Cloud (backend hosting), Cloudflare (domain, delivery and protection) and GoDaddy (transactional email delivery). Information may be disclosed if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of the service and its users.

7. Retention

Account details and content are kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete content or close your account, it is removed from active use, though limited copies may remain in backups for a period, and records we are required to retain, such as payment and tax records, are kept. Security logs are kept for a limited period.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for your account and content to be deleted, by writing to the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Children

The App and the service are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
