Privacy Policy

Bar Code Scanner  |  Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Bar Code Scanner mobile application ("the App") handles information. By installing or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

The App itself does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account system and the App operates no server of its own.

Barcode scanning is fully offline. Camera frames are analysed by a machine-learning model bundled inside the App and run entirely on your device. The content of any barcode you scan is never transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to the advertising service, and not attached to any advertising request.

The following is stored only on your device and is erased when you uninstall the App or clear its data:

  • Scan history: The codes you have scanned, kept so you can re-send any of them later.
  • Settings: Theme, send suffix (none / Tab / Enter), beep and vibration preferences.
  • Ad-free state: Which ad-removal tier you bought, and when a time-limited tier expires.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, occasional full-screen (interstitial) advertisements, an advertisement shown when you return to the app, and a rewarded advertisement you may choose to watch in exchange for 24 hours without ads.

AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers and usage information in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You can learn how Google uses advertising data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Barcode content, scan history and text typed into your PC are never shared with advertisers. If you buy an ad-removal tier, the App stops requesting advertisements.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The App receives only the identifier of the product you bought, so it knows which ad-free tier to apply. Your payment card details are handled entirely by Google and never reach us.

All purchases are one-time and never renew automatically. A previous purchase can be restored from within the App if you reinstall it.

4. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions. Each is used for one stated purpose:

  • Camera: Required. Used to read barcodes. Frames are processed on the device, are never stored, and are never transmitted.
  • Bluetooth (connect, scan, advertise): Required. Used to pair with your computer and send scanned codes to it as keyboard input over Bluetooth HID, a direct device-to-device link. No server is involved.
  • Vibrate: A short pulse to confirm a successful scan.
  • Internet and network state: Used only by Google AdMob to download and serve advertisements. Scanning and Bluetooth do not use them, and the App works in aeroplane mode.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, photos, files, SMS or call history.

5. Third-party services

Other than the advertising service described above, the App does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or any other third-party SDKs that collect data.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Because the App operates no server, we hold no data about you.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
