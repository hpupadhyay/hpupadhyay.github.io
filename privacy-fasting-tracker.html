Privacy Policy

Fasting & Water Tracker  |  Last updated: August 2026

Fasting & Water Tracker is an offline-first health utility. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud backend. Your fasting sessions, water log, weight log and settings are stored only on your device.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted by us.
  • No analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no tracking of any kind is included in the app.
  • The app never sends your health data (fasting, water, weight) anywhere.

Network features

The app has exactly two network features, and neither of them ever touches your health data:

  1. Advertising (Google AdMob) — banner and interstitial ads shown to free users. AdMob may use its own identifiers and collect data subject to Google's policies.
  2. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing) — optional "Buy Me a Coffee" support tiers that remove ads. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play.

Data storage & deletion

All app data lives in your device's private app storage. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) permanently deletes all entries. Because data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the effective date above will be revised.
