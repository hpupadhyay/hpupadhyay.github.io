Privacy Policy

SATTVA Launcher  |  Last updated: September 2026

SATTVA Launcher ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SATTVA Launcher mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

1. Overview

SATTVA Launcher is a 100% offline mindful launcher application. All your app usage data, screen time stats, and preferences are stored locally on your device. No internet connection is required, and no data is transmitted to external servers.

2. Information Collection and Use

SATTVA Launcher does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app functionality operates entirely on your device:

  • Usage Data: App usage statistics and screen time data are calculated locally on your device.
  • Preferences: Your launcher settings, home screen layout, and app organization are stored locally.
  • App Limits: Usage limits and focus modes are enforced locally on your device.

3. Permissions

SATTVA Launcher requests the following permissions:

  • Usage Access: Required to track app usage time for mindful usage statistics. This data is processed locally and never leaves your device.
  • Notifications: Required to show usage reminders and focus mode alerts.

4. Third-Party Services

SATTVA Launcher does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking software. There are no ads in this app.

5. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. No data is stored on any server or cloud service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data stays on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
