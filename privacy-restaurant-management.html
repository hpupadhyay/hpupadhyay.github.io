Privacy Policy

Restaurant POS  |  Last updated: September 2026

Restaurant POS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Restaurant POS mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

1. Overview

Restaurant POS is a 100% offline point-of-sale application. All your menu items, orders, billing data, and business records are stored locally on your device. No internet connection is required, and no data is transmitted to external servers.

2. Information Collection and Use

Restaurant POS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app functionality operates entirely on your device:

  • Menu Data: Your menu items, categories, and pricing are stored locally.
  • Order Data: All orders, billing records, and transaction history are stored locally in a SQLite database.
  • Business Data: Sales reports, daily summaries, and inventory data are calculated and stored locally.

3. Permissions

Restaurant POS requests the following permissions:

  • Storage: Required to export reports and backup data to your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Restaurant POS does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking software. There are no ads in this app.

5. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. No data is stored on any server or cloud service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data stays on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
