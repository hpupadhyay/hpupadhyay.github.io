Privacy Policy

Habit Tracker  |  Last updated: September 2026

Habit Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Habit Tracker mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

1. Overview

Habit Tracker is a 100% offline habit tracking application. All your habits, streaks, and progress data are stored locally on your device. No internet connection is required, and no data is transmitted to external servers.

2. Information Collection and Use

Habit Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app functionality operates entirely on your device:

  • Habit Data: Your habits, streaks, and completion history are stored locally in a SQLite database on your device.
  • Reminders: Habit reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Android's alarm system.

3. Permissions

Habit Tracker requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications: Required to send habit reminders. Reminders are processed locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Habit Tracker does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking software. There are no ads in this app.

5. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. No data is stored on any server or cloud service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data stays on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
